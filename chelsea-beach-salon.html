Our story

A hidden gem with a holiday state of mind.

Tucked inside Chelsea Farmers Market, Chelsea Beach Salon is a cool, retro, warm and friendly little world of its own. Step in off the King's Road and step into sunshine — fun decor, chatty stylists and results that turn heads.

Every member of our team has over seven years of experience, and our founder Antony has been perfecting his craft for more than twenty-five.

FAQ

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Call us on 020 3524 3899 or email salon@chelseabeach.co.uk.

Where exactly are you?+

We're at Unit 12, Chelsea Farmers Market, Chelsea, London SW3 6NR — a hidden gem just off the King's Road.

What are your opening hours?+

Monday to Saturday 09:00–19:30, and Sunday 11:00–17:00.

Do I need a patch test before colour?+

Yes — for your safety we ask for a hair patch test before colour services. It takes five minutes and is completely free.

Which products do you use?+

We colour and care with Davines, and use Shellac for long-lasting gel nails.

Do you cut children's hair?+

Absolutely. We offer wash, cut and blow dry for girls and boys under 10.

Can you do hair for weddings and events?+

Yes — from bridal hair to hair-ups and our signature “Chelsea Bun”. Our guests trust us for Ascot, weddings and every big day in between.
